NVIDIA cuDNN
============

This distribution includes NVIDIA cuDNN runtime libraries
(cudnn64_9.dll, cudnn_ops64_9.dll, cudnn_cnn64_9.dll, cudnn_adv64_9.dll,
cudnn_graph64_9.dll, cudnn_engines_precompiled64_9.dll,
cudnn_engines_runtime_compiled64_9.dll, cudnn_heuristic64_9.dll),
redistributed as part of the LibTorch (PyTorch) distribution.
